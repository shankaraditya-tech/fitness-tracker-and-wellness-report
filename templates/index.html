🧩 Four Pillars of Computational Thinking

1. Decomposition

Decomposed comprehensive health management into distinct functional modules: profile metrics, biometrics (BMI/BMR), daily logging, CSV file persistence, and report synthesis.

2. Pattern Recognition

Detected weekly activity streaks, workout type preferences, daily hydration consistency, and goal attainment rates across chronologically sorted CSV records.

3. Abstraction

Abstracted real-world physiological mechanics into standard mathematical formulations: Mifflin-St Jeor formula, WHO BMI indices, and exercise MET energy coefficients.

4. Algorithm Design

Authored step-by-step validation procedures, duplicate prevention filters, statistical mean aggregations, and automated Matplotlib headless chart generators.
